04 / Learning Style

How the Learning Happens

Take Apart

Disassemble the thing — physically or conceptually — to see how it actually works.

→

Map the System

Build a mental model, connecting parts to purpose.

→

Get Hands Dirty

Learn by building — the fastest route to real understanding.

→

Break It on Purpose

Stress-test the build to find where it actually holds up.

→

Rebuild Better

Fold the lessons back in and iterate toward elegance.

06 / Self-Assessment

Strengths & Current Challenges

⚡ Strengths

  • Comfortable moving between hardware, software, and creative/emotional writing without losing depth in any of them.
  • Strong systems thinking — sees how sail states, cannon logic, and networking all need to talk to each other before writing a line of code.
  • Genuine hands-on resourcefulness — salvaging and repurposing hardware rather than buying new.
  • Appreciates both the plain-language explanation and the precise technical term — learns fast because of it.

◈ Current Challenges

  • Juggling several ambitious builds at once (FleetCraft, LearnLore, SPARK, KENEYON-EL) means context-switching cost.
  • Turning early-stage R&D concepts into shippable, documented milestones.
  • Balancing deep technical scope (multiplayer networking, damage simulation) against realistic solo-dev bandwidth.
